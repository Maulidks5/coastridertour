# Coast Rider pricing benchmark

Last reviewed: 2026-11-01

Public market reference: [MB Tours public tour catalogue](https://mbtours.tz/api/tours)

## Pricing rule

- Public cards show only a genuine starting price and its basis, such as `per person · 4+ adults`.
- Zanzibar tour detail pages may show the complete group-size calculator.
- Tanzania safaris and Kilimanjaro show a planning price and require a confirmed quote.
- Tailor-made products remain quote-only.
- Never copy a competitor's number without matching transport, boat type, meals, entrance fees, taxes, pickup area and whether the trip is private or shared.
- Do not publish a new rate until direct costs and the target margin have been checked.

## Required cost check before changing a price

1. Vehicle and fuel cost from the covered pickup zone.
2. Boat or activity supplier charge.
3. Guide and crew payment.
4. Entrance, conservation and government fees.
5. Food, refreshments and equipment.
6. Payment, cancellation and weather-risk allowance.
7. Target gross margin.

Review public competitor rates and direct costs at least once each quarter and before peak-season pricing changes.
